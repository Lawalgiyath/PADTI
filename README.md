# PADTI (Professional Articulated Driver Training Institute)

PADTI is the world's premier institutional platform dedicated to the training, certification, and employment of articulated heavy vehicle drivers. It operates as a unified ecosystem connecting elite talent with the global logistics industry.

Mission
To professionalize articulated driving through rigorous institutional standards, high-performance simulation training, and a verified global marketplace for logistics talent and assets.

Core Ecosystem
- **Institutional Training**: FRSC-compliant curriculum meeting international licensing requirements (CDL Class A equivalent).
- **Career Marketplace**: A high-performance hub for Jobs, Verified Talent, Insurance, Equipment, and Services.
- **Credential Verification**: A secure gateway for employers to instantly validate driver certifications and performance scores.
- **AI Study Assistant**: Specialized GenAI tutor trained on PADTI certification material to help students master complex maneuvers and safety protocols.
- **Strategic Alignment**: Partnered with key stakeholders including the **Federal Road Safety Corp (FRSC)** and the **National Talent Export Programme (NATEP)**.

Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS, ShadCN UI.
- **Backend/Database**: Firebase Authentication (Email, Google, Phone), Cloud Firestore.
- **AI**: Google Genkit (Gemini 2.5 Flash) for institutional tutoring and platform assistance.
- **Analytics**: Recharts for performance and recruitment data visualization.

Stakeholders
- **Trainees**: Aspiring and professional drivers seeking global career mobility.
- **Employers**: Fleet owners and logistics giants sourcing verified excellence.
- **Partners**: Insurance providers, equipment manufacturers, and maintenance specialists.
- **Institutions**: Regulatory bodies and government agencies facilitating workforce development.
